## What you can do by tonight

- **DSA** — You can convert between binary and decimal and read a bit pattern without panic.
- **System design** — You can say what you would measure and what would page you at three in the morning.
- **C++ for contests** — You can use every bit trick the course needs without the shift that silently overflows, and you know the four builtins that replace a loop with one instruction.
- **Languages** — You can write the requirements and every API of the capstone before writing a line of logic.

## The questions today answers

- *What is 13 in binary? What does shifting left by one do?*
- *What metrics would you put on the dashboard for this system?*
- *What does 1 << 40 give you, and why?*
- *Design an e-commerce order platform.*
