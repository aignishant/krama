## What you can do by tonight

- **DSA** — You have a script for the first five minutes, so you never freeze at the start.
- **System design** — You can run requirements, estimation, API, data model, architecture and deep-dive in order.
- **Languages** — You can state the platform's throughput and latency and what you fixed to get there.

## The questions today answers

- *Talk me through your approach before you write any code.*
- *Design something. Show me your process, not just your answer.*
- *Your solution fails on test 14 of 60 and you cannot see the test. What do you do?*
- *What is your p99 latency, and what limits it?*
