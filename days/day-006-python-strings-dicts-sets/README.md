## What you can do by tonight

- **DSA** — You reach for a dict or a set by reflex when a problem asks whether you have seen something before.
- **System design** — You can explain what the padlock actually guarantees, and what it does not.
- **C++ for contests** — You can choose between map and unordered_map with a reason, and count, group and deduplicate anything.
- **Languages** — You can grow, shrink, index and copy a sequence in each language and say when a copy really happens.

## The questions today answers

- *How would you check for duplicates in O(n)?*
- *What does HTTPS protect you from? What does it not protect you from?*
- *When would you use std::map instead of std::unordered_map?*
- *What happens under the hood when you append to a full sequence?*
